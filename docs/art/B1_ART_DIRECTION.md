# B1 Cartoon Arcade art direction

This iteration prepares new original sprite production while preserving the approved Option A identity.

## Design
- Sigma: sharp, precise silhouette and distinct eye expression.
- Cool Kid: rounded, cap-led silhouette with energetic movement.
- Alpha Chad: broad, square silhouette and grounded poses.
- All characters: strong outlines, limited cel-shading, consistent pivots and sizes.
- Friendly Aura uses teal; hostile warnings use coral; special pickups use purple and distinctive shapes.
- New animations must follow actual game events, not alter gameplay timing or hitboxes.
- Ruin Alley remains the approved arena, without new collisions.

## B1 deliverables pending
- New transparent character sprites and animation sheets, with source files and provenance.
- Enemy silhouette comparison and arena contrast reference.
- Phaser previews and screenshots on desktop and mobile.
- Full TypeScript, Vitest, build and Playwright validation.
- Explicit creator review before any merge.

Existing approved artwork must not be overwritten. No paid services. This planning document alone does not complete B1.
